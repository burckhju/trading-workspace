# V1.2.0 – Trade Management

## Release

**Version:** `v1.2.0-trade-management`

**Feature:** FT-010 – Trade Management

**Merge baseline:** `18fe1df90e4f63529867fc32b5b6441d90738ff4`

## Scope

V1.2.0 extends the released FT-009 Trade & Position capability with active Trade Management.

Delivered capabilities include:

- BUY/SELL execution evolution
- immutable execution history and correction supersession
- deterministic Position reconstruction
- LONG-only partial and full exits
- Average Cost cost basis
- realized gross P&L
- CLOSED Position state
- immutable stop, target, thesis and management-note history
- management-event corrections
- current management-state projection
- REST contracts for active trade management
- dedicated Trade Management frontend
- composed trade timeline without duplicate sale truth
- FT-011 eligibility only after full economic exit
- historical sale capture independent of broker/provider availability
- backend integration and browser E2E coverage

## Architecture

The release preserves the approved FT-010 boundaries:

`Trade != ExecutionRecord != TradeManagementEvent != Position`

TradePlan and ProductSelection provenance remain immutable.

SELL remains the authoritative economic execution fact.

FT-010 remains LONG-only in V1.

Average Cost remains the single V1 cost-basis method.

Broker/order lifecycle and FT-011 post-trade implementation remain outside this release.
